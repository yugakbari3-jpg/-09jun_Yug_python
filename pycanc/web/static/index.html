Inside PyCanc

One low-dose CT in, six calibrated yearly risks out — no radiologist annotations, clinical data or prior scans needed at inference time. This replica reproduces the published network layer-for-layer, so the official MIT checkpoints load unchanged.

0.92 · 0.86 · 0.941-year AUC on NLST · MGH · CGMH test sets (paper)
0.75 · 0.81 · 0.806-year concordance index, same cohorts
37.4 Mparameters per ensemble member
5 ×checkpoints averaged + calibrated

Exact preprocessing

DICOM → HU → lung window (L −600 / W 1500) → 8-bit → 256² → normalise (x−128.17)/87.18 → resample to 0.703×0.703×2.5 mm → crop/pad to 200 slices, 3 channels.

3D ResNet-18 encoder

Video-pretrained r3d_18 with its classifier removed. A 200-slice volume becomes a 512×25×16×16 feature grid — each cell sees a ~22×22×20 mm neighbourhood.

Multi-attention pooling

Learned spatial attention inside each slice plus attention across slices yields the heat-maps shown in the viewer. A max-pool + Conv1d branch and a global max-pool are concatenated into one 1536-d vector.

Cumulative hazard layer

Non-negative yearly hazards are summed so predicted risk can never decrease with follow-up time.

zt = b(x) + Σi≤t ReLU(hi(x)),  P(cancer ≤ t) = σ(zt)

Ensemble + calibration

Five independently trained models are averaged; per-year Platt scaling followed by isotonic regression maps scores to calibrated probabilities.

Training signal

Trained on NLST with censored survival labels (masked BCE per year) plus attention guidance from radiologist-drawn nodule boxes on a subset — see pycanc/train.py.

About this replica

Sybil was developed by Peter Mikhael, Jeremy Wohlwend, Adam Yala, Regina Barzilay, Florian Fintelmann, Lecia Sequist and colleagues at MIT CSAIL / Jameel Clinic and Massachusetts General Hospital. This project rebuilds the model from the paper and the public reference code, and adds an interactive 2D/3D workstation around it.

Using the real weights

Click Get official weights in the top bar (or run python -m pycanc.cli download). The five MIT checkpoints and calibrators (~700 MB) load into this replica unchanged. Without them the network runs with untrained weights and its numbers are meaningless.

Your own scans

Zip a DICOM series folder and drop it on the workspace. NIfTI volumes and .npz files (hu, spacing) work too. Everything is processed locally on your machine.

Phantom generator

A procedural chest CT with vessels, airways, ribs, heart and a configurable nodule, so you can watch how size, density, margin and location move the risk and the attention.

Reference

Mikhael PG, Wohlwend J, Yala A, et al. Sybil: A Validated Deep Learning Model to Predict Future Lung Cancer Risk From a Single Low-Dose Chest Computed Tomography. J Clin Oncol 41:2191–2200, 2023.

Not for clinical use

Research and education only. Sybil was validated on screening LDCTs from specific populations; outputs on other scans, on phantoms, or without the official weights carry no clinical meaning.
